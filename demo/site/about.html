About us

Engineers working for leaner energy use

Verdance was founded on one belief: every company can reduce its energy footprint, as long as it has reliable figures and a realistic plan.

Our story

Founded in Lyon by two engineers from industry and construction, the firm first worked with workshops in the Rhône valley. Today our team of twelve engineers supports more than 240 sites, from logistics warehouses to office buildings.

Our mission has not changed: turn regulations and uncertainty into projects that are profitable, measured and lasting.

Our values

Measure before acting

No recommendation without data: every proposal rests on measurements and a profitability calculation.

Straight talk

We also tell you what is not worth doing. Our fees do not depend on the value of the works.

Stay until it works

We track real savings for a year after the works, and correct course if needed.
