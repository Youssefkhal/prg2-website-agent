Frequently asked questions

How much does an energy audit cost?

The first half-day diagnosis is free. The full audit is priced according to floor area and uses, and grants can cover a large share of it.

Do we have to stop production during the works?

No. We schedule work during quiet periods (weekends, holidays) and in phases to keep your business running.

Which companies do you work with?

Small and mid-sized industrial and commercial companies with sites of 1,000 to 40,000 m², mainly in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region.

How efficient is your building?

A 30-minute call is enough to spot your first savings opportunities.
